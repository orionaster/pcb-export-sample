The construction is the decision.

Layer count is the headline, but the material set, the drill and the finish decide what a board costs. Four constructions, one rule engine behind them.

Rigid multilayer

The default construction. FR-4 and high reliability laminates, one to four cores, through-hole only.

  • 1–32 layers
  • 0.10–0.60 mm hole
  • 1.6 mm nominal
  • ENIG / HASL / OSP
